`/delta off` turns Delta off for the current session: it sends what it already recorded, then stops recording the conversation and syncing its checkout, also after you resume the session. `/delta-share` turns it back on in the same thread and adds what was said while it was off, unless Claude compacted the conversation in between.

When Delta can't recover a session on its own, such as a checkout it refuses to restore or a Delta CLI too old to record with, its message now suggests `/delta off`.
