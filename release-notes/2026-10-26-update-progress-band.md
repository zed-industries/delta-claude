category: Improved
---

`/delta-update` and `/delta update` now show their progress on one animated line in Delta's bar above the prompt, with the version being installed and a progress bar while it downloads, instead of adding a transcript line for every step. The transcript keeps the start, the result, and, on Windows, the request to approve the installer's prompt, shown in full. When Delta is running agent work, the update now says to retry `/delta-update` once that work finishes.
