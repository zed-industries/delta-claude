The plugin now offers `/delta-setup` right after you install it, not only in your next session. Setup and `/delta-share` say what to type and where, call teams "teams" throughout, and stop up front with how to fix it when a repository has no usable Git remote. After you choose Private sync, setup offers to sync the current session too, and says why it isn't syncing if you decline.
